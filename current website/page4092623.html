Policy Information for All Parents
  • Trial Class
    You can sign up through the Parent Portal or by emailing us at info@bravorhythmic.com. Beginner trial classes are 55 minutes, and Xcel or Pre-Team trial classes are 1.5 hours. Wear something stretchy such as a t-shirt, leotard, shorts, or leggings, with socks or rhythmic shoes, and don’t forget a water bottle. If your child enjoys the class, you can easily enroll through the Parent Portal or by reaching out to us directly. Please note, we offer one free trial class per program. Additional trial classes can be taken for $40 per class.
  • Tuition
    Tuition is automatically charged on the 1st of each month using the card on file in the Parent Portal account.

    Monthly tuition is based on a four-week calendar. Tuition remains the same regardless of whether a month has additional class days or fewer class days due to scheduled breaks or holidays. Please note, we do not offer makeup class for days there are schedules breaks. We also do not refund or prorate tuition for missed classes due to personal travel.
  • Late Pick-Up
    To ensure the safety of our students and to respect our coaches’ time, Bravo Rhythmic has a late pick-up policy in place. Families are given a 5-minute grace period at the end of class. After the grace period, a late fee of $2 per minute will be applied until your child is picked up. We kindly ask all families to plan accordingly and arrive on time.
  • Pausing Enrollment
    If you plan to travel, please note that we are unable to place enrollments on hold. You may choose to cancel your enrollment and re-register upon your return; however, we cannot guarantee that your spot in the class will be available during your absence.
  • Makeup Classes
    A makeup token is automatically created in the Parent Portal when a class is missed. You can request a makeup class directly through the portal. Please note that there is a limit of one makeup token per month, and each token expires 30 days after it is issued.

    We do not offer makeup classes for scheduled breaks or holidays. Makeup classes are not offered for DVP/FIG and intermediate classes. Makeup tokens cannot be redeemed for a day of camp.
  • Cancellation Policy
    To cancel your enrollment, please email us at info@bravorhythmic.com at least two weeks in advance to avoid being charged for the following month.

    Please note: Xcel, DVP, and FIG programs run on an 11-month schedule from the beginning of August through the end of June. Memberships for these programs cannot be canceled before the end of June.
  • Gym Safety and Carpet Access
    For the safety of all our athletes, gymnasts may only be on the gym floor (carpet) when invited by a coach for their scheduled class. As a USA Gymnastics member club, our coaches are responsible for supervising children during their scheduled class time only. Before class begins and after class ends, children must remain under the supervision of a parent or guardian.

    We also kindly ask parents to keep a close eye on siblings and other young visitors during drop-off and pick-up. For everyone's safety, use of the gym floor, rhythmic equipment, or practicing gymnastics skills outside of coach supervision is not permitted.
  • Sportsmanship and Behavior
    We strive to provide a safe, positive, and respectful environment for every gymnast. Coaches are responsible for maintaining a productive class and expect gymnasts to listen, follow directions, and treat coaches and teammates with respect.

    If a gymnast's behavior becomes disruptive, disrespectful, or unsafe, coaches will provide reminders and opportunities to correct the behavior. If the behavior continues after two verbal warnings, the gymnast may be asked to sit out or leave practice early and parents may be contacted when appropriate.
For Competitive Teams
(Xcel, DVP & FIG)
  • Dress Code
    To maintain a focused and professional training environment, we ask that all competitive gymnasts follow a strict all-black dress code. Please wear fitted athletic clothing such as a black t-shirt or leotard with leggings or gym shorts. Hair must be neatly secured in a bun, and for safety reasons, large jewelry should be avoided. Rhythmic shoes are required for class.
  • Commitment and 11-month Policy
    The DVP, FIG and Xcel teams are all 11-month programs that start on August 1st and end in June 30th. It is required that gymnasts arrange their schedules to attend all scheduled practices, Bravo camps, and planned competitions.

    Active participation is crucial for the safety and success of all our athletes. We have a policy of not canceling memberships before the 11-month period, we can consider an exception in cases of relocation, medical reasons, or for first year competitive students.
  • Camp Participation
    For DVP/FIG athletes, participation in minimum one week of Summer Camp and minimum one week of Winter Camp is required. Camp fees will be automatically charged on July 1 (Summer Camp) and December 1 (Winter Camp).

    For Xcel athletes, participation in at least one week of camp each year is required.
  • Equipment and Uniforms
    Gymnasts in the competitive programs are expected to have their own set of rhythmic gymnastics equipment. During training we use various other equipment such as ankle weights and resistance bands which you will need to order. Gymnasts in the competitive teams will also be expected to order practice and competitive uniforms.
  • USA Gymnastics Membership
    All gymnasts who participate in competitions need to register as athlete members of USA Gymnastics and pay the associated membership fee. An invitation email or renewal reminder will be sent to you by a team manager.
  • Competition Expenses
    Parents of competing gymnasts are accountable for covering the costs associated with their coaches' participation in competitions, including travel expenses, such as transportation, food, and lodging. The fees and expenses of coaches are shared equally among all gymnasts attending the event.
  • Choreography
    As athletes progress to the next level, it is expected that most gymnasts will receive new choreography each year. Choreography fees are arranged directly between parents and coaches, and if a routine is shared by multiple gymnasts, the cost is divided among the families.
